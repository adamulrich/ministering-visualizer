Before you begin

Prepare the two source files

You will need one PDF from the current ministering assignments and one Excel workbook containing the latest unassigned lists.

1

Print the ministering route list

Go to LCR and open the ministering route list.

  1. Choose Print for the route list.
  2. In the print dialog, choose the Download button to save the PDF.
  3. Keep the PDF available for the upload step.
2

Capture the unassigned lists

Open the unassigned tab in LCR and capture both tables.

  1. Go to the Unassigned tab.
  2. Use the Table Capture browser plugin to copy the ministers and households tables.
  3. Put the two lists in separate Excel tabs named Unassigned Ministers and Unassigned Households.
Download a blank workbook template
Step two

Review readiness before placing anyone

When you import the PDF, assigned ministers and households appear on the diagram. The workbook supplies the unassigned lists for review.

◎

Review every minister

Work through the minister list and mark the people who are ready for assignment. Leave anyone who needs follow-up in Not ready.

H

Review every household

Choose an assignment preference for each household: Must, Should, Nice, or No. A practical order is to prioritize Must households first, then Should and Nice.

✓

Mark households ready

After deciding the preference, mark the households that are ready to place. This keeps the diagram focused on the people and households you are prepared to assign.

Step three

Build and balance the routes

Use the diagram as a working board. Drag people and households into routes, move routes between districts, and adjust the mix as you go.

Place people and households.Drag ready ministers and households from the left panels into a route.
Balance the districts.Drag a route to another district, or create a new route at the top of a district.
Use the route tools.🗺️ opens the route map, the padlock protects a route, and 📝 opens route notes.
Keep the board manageable.Use search, district filters, household-count filters, and household sorting to focus your view.
Step four

Save progress and continue later

Save often while you work. The downloaded progress workbook contains the diagram state, readiness decisions, route notes, and assignments.

Save progress

Click Save progress to download a new progress workbook. Keep that file as your working copy.

Load progress

Return later and use Load progress to restore the diagram and continue where you left off.

Refresh the unassigned lists

Upload a new unassigned workbook when the lists change. Existing records are matched, current decisions are preserved, and people no longer present are removed from the unassigned buckets.
